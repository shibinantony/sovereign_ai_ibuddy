# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, evidence-oriented community where people can challenge ideas without demeaning people.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome different backgrounds, disciplines, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Critique claims with evidence and state uncertainty openly.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Protect confidential and personal information.
- Credit others' work and respect licenses.
- Focus on the project's business, engineering, governance, and public-interest purpose.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory conduct;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing private information without permission;
- impersonation, misrepresentation, plagiarism, or license violation;
- using project channels to expose secrets, vulnerabilities, customer data, or restricted material;
- retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, releases, project events, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report ordinary conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through an available contact route on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If confidentiality is required and no other private route is available, use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel and label the report as a conduct matter.

Do not post sensitive details publicly. The maintainer may remove content, limit participation, reject contributions, issue a warning, temporarily or permanently ban participation, or refer credible threats or unlawful conduct to the appropriate service or authority.

Enforcement will consider context, impact, pattern, intent, remediation, and the safety of affected people. Retaliation is prohibited.
